# Payment Risk Policies & Guidelines

## 1. Risk Scoring & Decision Thresholds
- Risk Score Range: 0 to 100
- APPROVE (Score 0 - 30): Low risk transactions. Standard automated processing without manual intervention.
- REVIEW (Score 31 - 70): Medium risk transactions. Flagged for human analyst inspection and secondary verification.
- HOLD (Score 71 - 100): High risk transactions. Temporarily held to prevent potential fraudulent transfer while detailed investigation occurs.

## 2. Velocity Risk Policy
- High Velocity Alert: A customer attempting 3 or more transactions within 5 minutes is flagged as a high-velocity anomaly.
- Hourly Threshold: More than 8 transactions within 1 hour indicates automated script behavior or carding activity.
- Failed Attempt Spike: 3 or more consecutive failed payment attempts prior to a high-value transaction indicates potential credential stuffing or card testing.

## 3. Behavioral & Amount Anomaly Policy
- Amount Anomaly: A transaction amount greater than 3x the customer's historical average transaction amount triggers an amount deviation flag.
- Extreme Amount Anomaly: A transaction amount exceeding 5x historical average or over ₹100,000 / $1,500 for a new account (<30 days old) triggers immediate review.

## 4. Device & Location Anomaly Policy
- New Device Flag: First-time usage of a unique device identifier for an existing account.
- New Location Flag: Transaction initiated from a geographic location never previously recorded for the customer.
- Impossible Travel / Geo-Distance: A transaction originating from a location far from the customer's recent location within an impossibly short timeframe.

## 5. Chargeback History Policy
- Previous Chargebacks: Any customer with 1 or more previous chargebacks carries elevated baseline risk (+20 score adjustment).
- High Chargeback Risk: Customers with 2+ chargebacks require compulsory supervisor sign-off before releasing held transactions.
