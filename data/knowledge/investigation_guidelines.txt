# Fraud Investigation Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 1. Primary Investigation Workflow
When evaluating a transaction flagged as REVIEW or HOLD:
1. Verify Customer Identity & Account Age: Check if the account is new (<30 days) or established (>180 days). Established accounts with stable history have lower inherent risk.
2. Inspect Device & IP History: Compare the device fingerprint and IP location against past successful transactions.
3. Assess Transaction Velocity: Check if multiple small transactions were attempted prior to the current transaction (card testing pattern).
4. Evaluate Amount Relative to Baseline: Calculate the deviation between the current transaction amount and historical mean transaction amount.
5. Review Previous Chargeback & Dispute Records: Verify if the payment method or customer account has prior chargeback disputes logged.

## 2. Decision Matrix & Recommendations
- APPROVE Recommendation:
  - Evidence shows minor deviation (e.g. slight amount increase) but device and location match customer profile.
  - Customer responded positively to 2FA / OTP verification.
- MANUAL REVIEW / REQUEST VERIFICATION:
  - New device AND new location, but moderate transaction amount and no recent failed attempts.
  - Contact customer via registered phone or email to confirm authorization.
- HOLD / REJECT Recommendation:
  - Multiple velocity violations (>=3 transactions in 5 min) + new device + 3+ failed attempts + amount >4x baseline.
  - Previous confirmed chargebacks on record with matching device fingerprint.

## 3. Evidence Grounding Rules for Analysts
- Analysts and AI copilots must ground every recommendation strictly in verifiable telemetry data (logs, device IDs, transaction history).
- Never presume fraudulent intent without supporting anomaly evidence (e.g., velocity spikes, device mismatch, location mismatch).
- Always note potential false positives (e.g., legitimate user traveling or making a rare large purchase).
